# Janice C. Eberly

**Rolle:** James R. and Helen D. Russell Distinguished Professor of Finance, Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University; vormals Assistant Secretary for Economic Policy, U.S. Treasury (Obama-Administration). Forschungsfokus auf Investment-Theorie, Intangible Capital und Capital-Allocation.

Co-Autorin der JEP-Synthese *[[Crouzet-Eberly-Eisfeldt-Papanikolaou-2022-Economics-Intangible-Capital]]* — etabliert die zwei distinguishing properties Non-Rivalry + Limited-Excludability. Frühere Arbeit Crouzet/Eberly 2019 ist Reduced-Form-Vorgänger.
