# Kewei Hou

**Rolle:** Professor of Finance, Ohio State University, Fisher College of Business. Forschung zu Empirical Asset Pricing, Anomalien, q-Theory of Investment.

Co-Autor von *[[Hou-Xue-Zhang-2015-q-Factor]]* — RFS 28(3) 650-705 "Digesting Anomalies: An Investment Approach". 4-Faktor q-Modell (MKT + ME + I/A + ROE) als Alternative zum Carhart-4-Faktor und FF-5-Faktor; outperformt in Pricing von 7+ Anomalien.
