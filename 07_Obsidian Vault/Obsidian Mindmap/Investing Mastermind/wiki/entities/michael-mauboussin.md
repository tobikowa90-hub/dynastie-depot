# Michael J. Mauboussin

**Rolle:** Head of Consilient Research, Morgan Stanley Counterpoint Global. Adjunct Professor of Finance, Columbia Business School. Bekannter Autor zu Value Creation, Decision-Making, Behavioral Finance ("Expectations Investing", "More Than You Know", "The Success Equation").

Co-Autor von *[[Mauboussin-Callahan-2024-Measuring-Moat]]* — 3. Auflage, Oktober 2024. Systematisiert Moat-Bewertung um Magnitude (ROIC-WACC-Spread × Investment-Größe) + Sustainability (CAP). Erweitert Morningstar-Taxonomie um zeitliche Dimension.
