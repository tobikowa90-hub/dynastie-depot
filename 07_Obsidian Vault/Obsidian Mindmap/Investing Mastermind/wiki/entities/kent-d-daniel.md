# Kent D. Daniel

**Rolle:** William von Mueffling Professor of Business, Columbia Business School. Vormals Goldman Sachs Asset Management Quantitative Investment Strategies. Forschungsfokus Behavioural-Finance, Asset-Pricing-Anomalien und Faktor-Modelle. Bekannt für Daniel-Hirshleifer-Subrahmanyam (1998, JF) "Investor Psychology and Security Market Under- and Overreactions".

Co-Autor von *[[Daniel-Hirshleifer-Sun-2020-Behavioural-Factors]]* — RFS 33(4) 1673-1736 — DHS-3-Faktor-Behavioural-Modell (MKT+PEAD+FIN) als Alternative zu Fama-French-Multi-Faktor-Modellen.
