Keyence Corporation’s consolidated financial results for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026, recorded net sales of 1,169,289 million yen (up 10.4% year-on-year), operating profit of 595,759 million yen (up 8.4% year-on-year), ordinary profit of 635,756 million yen (up 13.3% year-on-year), and net profit attributable to owners of the parent of 445,185 million yen (up 11.7% year-on-year), achieving increases in both sales and profits. Earnings per share (EPS) increased to 1,835.63 yen (from 1,643.77 yen in the previous fiscal year). Against the backdrop of a robust financial base, the company has decided to set the year-end dividend at 275 yen (compared to 175 yen for the previous fiscal year) and increase the annual dividend to 550 yen from 350 yen in the previous fiscal year. The annual dividend forecast for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027, is also set at 550 yen. The equity ratio remains at a high level of 94.6%.
