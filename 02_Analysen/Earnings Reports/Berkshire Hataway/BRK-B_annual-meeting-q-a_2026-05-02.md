## ERÖFFNUNG & ZEREMONIE

Greg Abel eröffnete das Meeting mit einer symbolischen Geste: Warren Buffetts Trikot mit der Nummer „60" wurde unter dem Applaus der Aktionäre unter das Hallendach des CHI Health Centers gehängt — direkt neben dem Trikot des verstorbenen Charlie Munger (Nummer „45"). Buffett ergriff kurz das Mikrofon: „We couldn't have made a better decision. That's been 100% successful. [He's doing] everything I did and then some. He's the right person."

Buffett lobte auch Apple-CEO Tim Cook, der in Kürze abgelöst wird: „How would you like to step into the shoes of Steve [Jobs] and come through his record? It's one of the miracles of American business management. … Thank you, Tim." Er verwies auf Berkshires Apple-Investment: „When Steve died and when we made our investment and turned over 10% of the resources of Berkshire, we were turning it over to Tim, and he has turned that into $185 billion or something pretax."

## BECKY QUICK INTERVIEW — BUFFETT SIDELINE (Mittagspause)

### Q1 — Investitionsumfeld + Rekord-Cash

**A — Buffett:**
> „It isn't our ideal surrounding area — or environment, I should say — in terms of deploying cash for Berkshire."

Berkshire habe „right management", könne „pick our spots". Bewertungs-Zurückhaltung.

> „**When nobody else will answer their phones**" — Markteinbruch als Kaufsignal.

→ **Cash-Pile-Treatment-Anchor #2 (Buffett-Stimme):** disziplinierte antizyklische Position. Kein Score-Lift, NEUTRAL.

### Q2 — Marktkultur Investing vs. Gambling

**A — Buffett:**
Märkte als „a church with a casino attached":
> „People can move between the church and the casino, and I would say there are more people in the church [than] people in the casino, but the casino has gotten very attractive."

> „If you're buying one-day options or selling them, that's not investing, it's not speculating – it's gambling."

> „We've never had people in a more gambling mood than now."

US-Soldaten-Insider-Trading-Beispiel ($400k auf Prediction Markets, Venezuela-Operation): „The quantity of those things is just incredible."

→ **Markt-Bewertungs-Frame-Signal sehr defensiv** — bestärkt Cash-ATH-Methodology.

### Q3 — Kernbotschaft

**A — Buffett:**
> „If the whole world lived by the Golden Rule, it would be such a more wonderful society. ... It doesn't cost you anything. In fact, it's reflected in better behavior toward you."

„Not a religious guy", aber kein Prinzip in „a couple thousand years" besser formuliert.
